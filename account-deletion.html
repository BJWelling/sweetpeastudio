Account and Data Deletion

SweetPea Baby Tracker · SweetPea Studio · Updated November 10, 2026

If you created a SweetPea Baby Tracker account, you can request deletion of your account and data associated with it without reinstalling the App.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to codejoltz@gmail.com with the subject SweetPea Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address or account identifier you used for SweetPea, if applicable. Do not send passwords or sensitive information about your child.
  3. We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership. We will then explain and process the deletion request according to the actual account configuration.

Request account deletion by email

What can be deleted

We will delete account information and associated data that SweetPea Studio actually holds, subject to any legally required retention. Based on the App's stated local-only configuration, baby tracking records are saved on your device and are not accessible to us remotely. You can delete local entries through available App controls or remove the App's local storage using your device settings. Device backups managed by your operating-system provider may require separate deletion.

Timing and retained data

We will respond to your request and explain how your account and any associated data will be deleted. If any information must be retained to comply with applicable law, we will explain the reason and the relevant retention period.

Questions

Contact SweetPea Studio at codejoltz@gmail.com. See our Privacy Policy.
