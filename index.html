Privacy Policy

SweetPea Baby Tracker · SweetPea Studio
Effective date: November 10, 2026

SweetPea Baby Tracker ("SweetPea", "the App") helps parents and caregivers keep track of a child's care routines, growth, and milestones. This policy explains the information the App handles, how it is used, and how you can manage it.

Local-first approach. Based on the App's current intended configuration, records are stored on your device rather than transmitted to a SweetPea server. Please read the account and device-backup sections for important distinctions.

1. Who operates the App

SweetPea Baby Tracker is provided by SweetPea Studio. For privacy questions or requests, email codejoltz@gmail.com.

2. Information you choose to enter

Depending on the features you use, the App may access and store information such as:

  • Child profiles: name, birth date, sex, profile picture, and birth measurements.
  • Feeding: breastfeeding, bottle-feeding, pumping, solid-food entries, amounts, times, and notes.
  • Care routines: sleep sessions, diaper changes, and other daily activity logs.
  • Growth and health-related entries: height or length, weight, head circumference, temperature, and associated dates and notes.
  • Memories: milestones, descriptions, and photos you choose to add.
  • Preferences: measurement units, display and reminder settings, and other app preferences.
  • Account-related entries: any identifiers or credentials you provide for account functionality, if supported by the installed version.

You decide which optional details to enter. Child information may be sensitive, and you should enter only information you are comfortable keeping on your device.

3. How information is used

The App uses this information to display records, calculate summaries, create growth charts, show care history, maintain your preferences, and provide the reminders or notifications you enable. We do not use child information for targeted advertising or sell it to data brokers.

4. Storage, collection, and transmission

Under the local-only configuration described to us, the tracking records and settings you enter are stored on your device, and SweetPea Studio does not receive copies of those records through an App-operated backend. The App is not intended to transmit tracking records to analytics or advertising providers.

Having an account option does not, by itself, establish whether authentication details stay on the device or are processed remotely. If sign-in, account recovery, backup, synchronization, caregiver invitations, or other features send information to a server or third-party provider, this policy must be updated to identify the information, recipients, purposes, and retention before those features are released.

Your device operating system or backup settings may separately back up app data or photos through services you control. Such backups are subject to the relevant device or backup provider's settings and policies.

5. Sharing and service providers

Based on the declared local-only configuration, we do not intentionally share the tracking records entered in the App with advertisers, analytics providers, or other external recipients. If you choose to export or share information through features on your device, the destination and any recipient you select may process that information under their own policies.

We do not claim that all operating-system services or dependencies are offline. We will update this section if verification of the released App identifies external processing.

6. Permissions and notifications

The App may request permission to send notifications and, if you use image features, access photos or a photo picker. Notifications are used for reminders or active-tracking information. Photo access, where available, is used for images you choose to attach. Available permissions depend on the installed version and your device. You can manage permissions in your device settings.

7. Security

We aim to minimize transmission of sensitive records by keeping tracking information locally on the device. Protection of locally stored data also depends on your device's security controls, including screen locking, operating-system protections, and backup settings. No method of storing information is completely risk-free. We do not represent that local data is encrypted by the App unless such encryption is implemented and verified.

8. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on the device until you delete them using available App controls or remove the App's stored data. Uninstalling the App normally removes its local app data, but operating-system or device backups may retain copies according to their own settings.

If you have an account, you can ask us to delete it and any data associated with it by following the instructions on our Account and Data Deletion page. An email request is available even if you no longer have the App. We may need information to verify that the request relates to your account. We will explain any data that we cannot directly delete because it exists only on your device or in a backup controlled by you.

If an online account service is added, associated server-side records will also be covered by the deletion process, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

9. Children’s privacy

The App is intended for use by parents and caregivers, not for independent use by children. Parents or caregivers choose what information about a child to enter. The App is not designed to collect information directly from children, and child records are used only for the care-tracking functions described here.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

You can choose which optional information to record and can use the App's available controls to update or delete entries. Depending on your location, applicable privacy laws may give you additional rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we process. Contact codejoltz@gmail.com for assistance. We cannot remotely access records that are stored only on your device.

11. International processing

The local-only configuration does not intentionally transfer tracking records to SweetPea-operated servers in another country. If later versions introduce online services or international transfers, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant processing and safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be available at this URL with a revised effective date. Where applicable, we will provide additional notice or seek consent.
